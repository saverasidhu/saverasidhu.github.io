About

Building a career around people, systems and impact.

I am a Psychology and Criminology student at the University of Auckland with a strong interest in the relationship between people, institutions, technology and social change.

My work has taken me from youth wellbeing workshops in schools to international conversations on children's digital rights and AI governance. Across these experiences, I have become particularly interested in how evidence, policy and advocacy can work together to create better outcomes for young people.

Alongside university, I have developed leadership through competitive karate, public speaking, debating, education and community service.

I am particularly interested in the future intersection of psychology, criminology and law.

Areas of interest
Youth Wellbeing Prevention, resilience, confidence and accessible wellbeing education.
Technology & AI Governance Children's digital rights, AI literacy and meaningful youth participation in technology policy.
Psychology & Research Understanding behaviour and using evidence to improve real-world outcomes.
Law & Public Policy Exploring how legal and policy systems can promote fairness, protection and social change.

Featured initiative
Bright Start
Founder & Executive Director · Youth Wellbeing

Bright Start is a youth wellbeing initiative focused on equipping young people with practical tools for confidence, resilience and emotional wellbeing. The initiative was built around a simple principle: young people should receive support before problems become crises.

160+ Rangatahi reached through youth wellbeing workshops.
1,200+ Signatures supporting preventative mental health education in primary schools.
15-page Educator toolkit created to help make wellbeing education practical and accessible.
1,000+ Online engagement generated through youth wellbeing advocacy and educational content.
